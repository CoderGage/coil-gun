# Coil Gun

Here I'll be documenting the creation of my coil gun. I am following a project guide, which is the project-guide.pdf file. Credits to Simon Monk for writing the book that this guide is from. 

## About the Gun
The coil gun is just a lot of components mounted on a wood board, which is shaped to look and behave like a gun. It is powered by four 9-volt batteries and has a charging and firing mode controlled by the momentary "trigger" switch. The original project utilizes an old plastic pen chamber as the barrel, and plastic pieces cut from food containers to hold the coil together. Instead, I will be designing and 3d printing the barrel and coil retainers so that they can be easily modified to suit the gun more and add an aspect of creativity since I am following a guide. Once the gun is built, I plan to test it and modify it to improve it and customize it to my liking. 

## How it Works
This coil gun has capacitors, which can store lots of power and discharge (release) it quickly. The capacitors are first charged by the batteries, then their power is released into the coil when the gun is fired. The coil is simply a bunch of wound wire, which, when powered, creates a magnetic field to propel the projectile. The gun utilizes the charging of capacitors and the discharge of large amounts of power into a coil, which can electromagnetically propel a magnetic projectile forward. This works because the coil, which gains lots of power from the power discharged from the capacitors, quickly attracts the projectile to the center of the coil with lots of velocity. The coil then quickly loses its power, and the projectile uses its momentum from the quick attraction to propel itself outside of the firing chamber.
